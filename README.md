# Recruitment Policy and Benefit Analysis of Islami Bank Bangladesh Ltd.

##  Project Overview
This repository contains a comprehensive Human Resource Management (HRM) study on **Islami Bank Bangladesh Limited (IBBL)**. IBBL is the first and largest private Shariah-compliant Islamic bank in Bangladesh, established in 1983. 

The primary objective of this project is to explore and evaluate the real-world strategic HRM practices employed by IBBL to manage its workforce effectively and ethically.

###  Key Areas Analyzed
1. **Recruitment & Selection Process:** Evaluation of both internal and external hiring channels, third-party screening processes (Bankers Selection Committee), and multi-tier interviews to ensure transparency and bias-free selection.
2. **Training & Development:** Analysis of foundational Off-the-Job training, specialized On-the-Job training (job rotations, special assignments), and the Executive Development Programme (EDP) for senior leadership.
3. **Benefits & Compensation Structures:** Detailed breakdown of employee satisfaction drivers, financial benefits (Provident Fund with 9% yearly increment projections, Gratuity scales, and insurance schemes), and non-financial incentives like medical welfare and family vacations.

##  Methodology & Sources
* **Primary Research:** Conducted a structural online interview with **Zahir Mohammad Alam**, Principal Officer at the Head Office of Islami Bank Bangladesh Ltd.
* **Secondary Research:** Utilized data points extracted from the official IBBL web infrastructure and corporate Annual Reports.

##  Key Strategic Recommendations Included
* Establishing a more robust, formalized executive succession framework.
* Expanding targeted workplace diversity practices to accommodate minority groups and bring fresh perspectives.
* Introducing specialized mental health initiatives to enhance employee work-life balance.
* Increasing investment in AI and machine learning to optimize time management and automate routine transactional workloads.
